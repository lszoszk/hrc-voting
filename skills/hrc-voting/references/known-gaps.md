# Known gaps in the OHCHR catalogue (snapshot v1.2.0, harvest of 26 September 2026)

The Commission and Council records come from the OHCHR Library "Voting" collection, harvested
completely (6,392 records = the collection's own total). "Complete" means complete against that
catalogue, not against everything the Council decided. For sessions 43–59 (2020–2025) the
catalogue was checked against the Council's official session reports (`A/HRC/<session>/2`).
Symbols and outcomes agree, except for the items below, which the catalogue does not hold and
this dataset therefore lacks. Say so whenever an answer touches these sessions.

## Rejected draft texts the catalogue lacks (2 of the Council's 3)
- A/HRC/48/L.11 (Yemen, technical assistance and capacity-building): rejected (18 Yes, 21 No, 7 abstentions; A/HRC/48/2).
- A/HRC/51/L.6 (draft decision: debate on the situation in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region): rejected (17 Yes, 19 No, 11 abstentions; A/HRC/51/2).
- The third, A/HRC/59/L.1/Rev.1 (discontinuing the Eritrea Special Rapporteur's mandate), IS in the dataset (4 Yes, 25 No, 18 abstentions).

## Amendments the catalogue lacks (21)
- A/HRC/48/L.49–L.57 to draft L.21/Rev.1: L.50 and L.52–L.57 rejected by recorded vote; L.49 and L.51 withdrawn.
- A/HRC/49/L.43: rejected by recorded vote (24–13, 7 abstentions).
- Session 56 (11): L.43 rejected (25–3, 17 abstentions); L.27, L.50, L.52–L.56 withdrawn; L.28 not considered (no Council member co-sponsored it); L.37 and L.38 (to draft L.4): the report records no action.

## Votes that are not what they look like
- A/HRC/26/L.37 (2014) and A/HRC/29/L.37 (2015) record votes on a motion that no action be taken on an amendment. A "Yes" there means in favour of taking no action.
- Other procedural votes are not catalogued.

## Timing
- Recent sessions reach the catalogue some weeks after they close, so the newest session can be missing or incomplete. The snapshot includes session 62 (June–July 2026).

## Verified totals from the session reports (for fact-checking claims about HRC amendments)
Amendments tabled, by year: 2020 58 · 2021 103 · 2022 85 · 2023 68 · 2024 45 (359 in 2020–2024).
Outcomes 2020–2024: 207 rejected, 122 withdrawn, 23 not acted upon (no member requested action), 5 adopted
(A/HRC/48/L.59, 48/L.60, 50/L.31, 52/L.58, 52/L.59), 2 with no recorded action. The snapshot dates 340
amendments to 2020–2024 (58, 94, 84, 69, 35 by year) against the reports' 359: the 21 listed above are missing,
and a couple of catalogue entries (e.g. A/HRC/53/L.47) are not mentioned in the reports. When a published
figure differs from these, the session report is the reference, not the catalogue.
